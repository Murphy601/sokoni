# Sokoni payment methods

## Accepted
- **M-Pesa STK push** — the default. Buyer gets a prompt and enters their PIN.
- **M-Pesa Buy Goods till** — fallback when STK fails. The buyer then replies *paid* with the M-Pesa code for verification.

Both land in Sokoni escrow, not in the seller's pocket.

## Not accepted
- Cash on delivery is not available on Sokoni. Every order is prepaid.
- Paying a rider directly, in cash or by M-Pesa, is never part of a Sokoni order. A rider asking for money is a red flag — buyers should report it in chat.
- Sending money to a personal number or a till a stranger shares in chat is never a Sokoni payment.

## Answering a buyer who wants cash on delivery
Acknowledge the worry, then explain the swap: they are not being asked to trust the seller, they are being asked to trust escrow. Their money sits with Sokoni until they have the parcel and are happy with it, and it comes back to them if the item is wrong. That is a stronger position than cash, because cash paid at the door is gone.

## Fees a buyer sees
Item price, seller shipping for their area, and a banded M-Pesa transaction fee on the order total. Nothing is collected at the door.

## Safety
Sokoni never asks for an M-Pesa PIN. Never invent a till number — only the STK prompt or the till shown at checkout is valid.
