# Shipping & delivery (sellers + buyers)

## Buyers
- After prepaid M-Pesa escrow, the seller dispatches via Sokoni Mashinani hubs or their configured courier/rider.
- Track with your SKN-#### (or older SK-####). Rider contact appears when the seller has dispatched with rider details.
- Damaged / wrong item: reply HELP SKN-#### — escrow is held; send photos for support.

## Sellers — set delivery prices
1. Seller Hub → Shipping Rates.
2. Add zones: e.g. Nairobi / local CBD = KES 300; Upcountry Kenya = KES 500.
3. Save. Quotes at checkout use these zones automatically.
4. Hub drop-offs and rider pickup are managed under Hub Drop-Offs in Seller Hub.

## What the AI can do
- Explain steps and read your saved shipping profile (if your WhatsApp matches the seller account).
- It will not invent rates. Mutations go through Seller Hub (authenticated).
