# Rider onboarding (how to become a Sokoni rider)

Riders apply **on the website**, not by messaging support. There is a real form.

## Two ways to apply — both end in the same ops queue

**On WhatsApp (offer this first).** Reply *RIDER APPLY* and Sokoni walks through
every field in chat, with ID, licence and stage letter sent as ordinary photos.
Most riders have WhatsApp and no data bundle for a web form.

**On the web.** https://sokonimall.com/boda/apply.html

## What the form asks for
Required:
- Full name
- WhatsApp / M-Pesa phone (the same number is used for job offers and payouts)
- National ID number
- Operating town — **Nairobi or Thika only** right now
- Base stage / location
- Motorbike registration plate
- National ID photo (front)
- Valid driving licence, Class A
- Stage chairman recommendation letter

Optional, but speeds up vetting:
- National ID back
- Logbook / power of attorney
- Good Conduct certificate (DCI)
- NTSA badge / registration

## After submitting
Sokoni ops reviews the documents and replies on WhatsApp. Usually within 24
hours. Status goes `PENDING` → `VERIFIED` (or `REJECTED` with a reason).

Once verified, jobs are **auto-assigned** by the dispatch engine — riders do not
browse or pick from a list. See `rider-delivery.md` for the commands used on a
live job.

## Answering someone who wants to join
Give them the apply link and the required documents. Do not tell them to message
Sokoni support to apply: they are already messaging Sokoni, so that is a dead
end, and the form is the only thing that creates an application record.

Never promise approval, earnings figures, or a review time shorter than 24 hours.

## Applying on WhatsApp

The whole application can be completed in chat — no website needed, which
matters for riders without data. Any of **ride**, **rider**, **boda** or
**RIDER APPLY** starts it. Offer that first; the web form at
https://sokonimall.com/boda/apply.html is the alternative, not the default.

The phone number must be Kenyan. Payouts go out by M-Pesa, so a non-Kenyan
number cannot be paid and will be refused at the phone step.

## Stage pin — optional

The stage step also asks for a map pin. Offers go to the riders nearest a
pickup, so a pin means more nearby jobs. It can be skipped and the application
still completes — a rider without one is matched on their live location or the
stage name instead. See `location-pins.md`.
