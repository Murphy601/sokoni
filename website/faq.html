No. Sokoni Mall is 100% prepaid for local catalog items. You pay upfront via M-Pesa before we dispatch. Your money stays in escrow until delivery is confirmed — then the seller is paid.

You pay the full item price upfront — that is the prepaid model. We do not charge hidden commitment fees on top. The listed price is what you pay into escrow before dispatch.
