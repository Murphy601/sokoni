Frequently Asked Questions

Sokoni Mall · sokonimall.com

What is Sokoni Mall?

Kenya's prepaid marketplace for brand new and pre-loved fashion and lifestyle items. Shop on the website or WhatsApp — pay upfront, track with SK-####, and get escrow protection until delivery.

How does shopping on WhatsApp work?

No app or account needed. Message Sokoni Plug on +254 117 422 428 in English, Kiswahili, or Sheng. Search the local catalog, place a prepaid order, pay via M-Pesa, and track in the same chat.

Do I pay on delivery (COD)?

No. Sokoni is 100% prepaid. You pay the full item price upfront before dispatch. Funds stay in escrow until delivery is confirmed — then the seller is paid.

How do I pay?

After ordering you'll get an SK-#### reference. Pay via M-Pesa STK push (enter your PIN when prompted) or manually to Buy Goods Till 4775847 (registered to founder David Thuku Muiruri) using your order number as the reference. Reply paid in chat if you paid manually so we can verify.

Why does the Till show a personal name?

During our founder-led launch, Till 4775847 is registered to David Thuku Muiruri — a named individual, not an anonymous account. A formal business till transition is in progress. Always verify your SK-#### on WhatsApp before manual payment.

What is escrow protection?

Your payment is held by Sokoni until the item is delivered and confirmed. If something goes wrong (wrong item, not as described), contact us within 48 hours — eligible prepaid orders may qualify for refund from escrow.

How do I track my order?

Every order gets an ID like SK-1042. Track on sokonimall.com/track.html or type your order number / track in WhatsApp for the live shipment timeline (label → hub → in transit → delivered).

Can I buy international items (AliExpress, Temu, Amazon)?

No. Sokoni lists local Kenya inventory only — brand new and pre-loved items from sellers on our platform. We do not route to overseas marketplaces or handle import duties.

How do I sell on Sokoni?

Go to sokonimall.com/suppliers/list — upload 1–4 photos, add title, price, and condition, then post. Listings can go live instantly and are subject to moderation. Sellers drop off at a hub with an SK label; payout releases after confirmed delivery.

What offers or discounts are available?

During beta, use code SOKONI3 in WhatsApp chat for up to 3% off eligible prepaid orders. Promotional caps may change — we will never ask for deposits or fees on top of the listed price.
