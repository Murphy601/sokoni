About Sokoni Mall

Kenya's #1 prepaid marketplace · Founded by David Thuku Muiruri · sokonimall.com

Sokoni Mall Enterprise is a Kenyan peer-to-peer marketplace for brand new merchandise and pre-loved fashion. We combine a Depop-style storefront at sokonimall.com with a smart WhatsApp assistant — so you can browse, pay upfront, and track every order without downloading another app.

Our mission

Make trusted local commerce feel as easy as texting a friend. We process requests in English, Kiswahili, and Sheng, match buyers to real listings across Kenya, and hold every payment in escrow until delivery is confirmed.

How Sokoni works

  • Shop — browse on the website or chat with Sokoni Plug on WhatsApp.
  • Pay upfront — 100% prepaid via M-Pesa STK push (Daraja) or verified till payment. No cash on delivery.
  • Escrow protection — your money is held safely until the item arrives as described.
  • Track — every order gets an SKN-#### code. Follow shipment from label to delivered on track.html or in WhatsApp.

Sell on Sokoni

Anyone can list items at sokonimall.com/suppliers/list — upload photos, add details, and post live. Sellers drop off at a hub using a prepaid label; payouts release after confirmed delivery. Zero listing fees during launch.
