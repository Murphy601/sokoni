Slide 3 · Earnings

How and when you get paid

Payout trigger

CONFIRM delivery OTP → ~15 minute hold (buyer can dispute) → fee clear / M-Pesa B2C when configured.

WhatsApp ledger pings

After jobs, Sokoni texts fee status updates. Keep the same WhatsApp / M-Pesa number you registered with.

Honest fee note

Platform takes a small cut of the delivery fee (about 10%) plus M-Pesa B2C tariff where applicable — so the wallet amount can be slightly under the gross fee shown on the offer. High single-job fees may need ops approval before auto-send.
